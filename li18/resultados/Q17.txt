LI18 | Johnny Suarez Abasto | ¿Cuál es el ingreso total por año y mes?
Fecha y hora del sistema: 2026-10-27T20:08:08-04:00
SQL EJECUTADO
 SELECT year(from_unixtime(unix_timestamp(InvoiceDate, 'M/d/yyyy H:mm'))) AS anio,
month(from_unixtime(unix_timestamp(InvoiceDate, 'M/d/yyyy H:mm'))) AS mes, ROUND(SUM(Quantity * UnitPrice), 2)
AS ingreso_total FROM db3_john.data3_john WHERE Quantity > 0 AND UnitPrice > 0 GROUP BY
year(from_unixtime(unix_timestamp(InvoiceDate, 'M/d/yyyy H:mm'))),
month(from_unixtime(unix_timestamp(InvoiceDate, 'M/d/yyyy H:mm'))) ORDER BY anio, mes;
